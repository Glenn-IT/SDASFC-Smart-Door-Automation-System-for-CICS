Crucial Beginner Warnings Before Powering On:

1. RC522 RFID Must Connect to 3.3V ONLY: Connecting RC522 to 5V will permanently burn out the RFID chip.
2. Calibrate Buck Converter First: Turn the brass potentiometer screw on the LM2596 until your multimeter reads exactly 5.0V before connecting to the ESP32.
3. Common Ground Is Mandatory: All module GND pins (Power Supply, Buck, ESP32, Relay, Sensor, RTC, DFPlayer) must connect together.
4. Inline 1kΩ Resistors for DFPlayer: Insert a 1kΩ resistor on ESP32 GPIO 17 ➔ DFPlayer RX and DFPlayer TX ➔ ESP32 GPIO 16 to eliminate static clicking noise.

📋 Step-by-Step Assembly Checklist
  • 1
    Calibrate LM2596 Buck Converter

    Connect 12V to Buck VIN. Turn potentiometer screw until VOUT measures exactly 5.0V.

  • 2
    Wire Common Ground & 5V Rails

    Connect Buck 5V to ESP32 VIN, Relay VCC, RTC VCC, and DFPlayer Pin 1. Tie all GNDs together.

  • 3
    Connect RFID-RC522 Module (3.3V Only!)

    Connect RC522 3.3V to ESP32 3V3. Connect SS➔G5, SCK➔G18, MOSI➔G23, MISO➔G19, RST➔G4.

  • 4
    Connect DS3231 RTC Clock

    Connect SDA to GPIO 21, and SCL to GPIO 22.

  • 5
    Wire DFPlayer Mini & 3W Speaker

    GPIO 17 ➔ 1kΩ ➔ RX (Pin 2). TX (Pin 3) ➔ 1kΩ ➔ GPIO 16. SPK1 & SPK2 to 3W speaker.

  • 6
    Wire Relay, IR Exit Sensor & 12V Lock

    ESP32 GPIO 27 to Relay IN. IR Exit NO to GPIO 33. Relay NO/COM switches 12V to Door Lock.
