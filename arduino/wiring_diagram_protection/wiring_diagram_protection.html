SDASFC Door Lock Anti-Crash & Wiring Guide

Where and how to connect the 1N4007 Flyback Diode, 1000µF Filter Capacitor, and Relay Optical Isolation to permanently eliminate ESP32 serial corruption and brownout reboot loops when tapping the Master Card.

💥 What Happened on your Serial Monitor:
When other cards were tapped, access was denied so the relay never switched ON. But when the Master Card was tapped, the ESP32 commanded the relay to unlock the door immediately: [DOOR] >>> 🔓 RELAY ENERGIZErƨ E8rX ^&屧f dpRBbcb~&... rst:0x1 (POWERON_RESET) Switching the 12V lock created a massive inductive reverse spike (Back-EMF) and collapsed the 5V rail (Brownout), causing the ESP32 CPU registers to glitch and restart.
✅ The 3-Step Permanent Hardware Solution:
1. 1N4007 Flyback Diode: Clamped directly across the 12V Lock wires to swallow inductive back-EMF in <1 microsecond.
2. 1000µF 16V Capacitor: Positioned across the 5V and GND rail to buffer against inrush voltage dips.
3. Common Ground & Buck Calibration: Solid shared GND and 5.0V regulated power ensures rock-steady operation.

1
1N4007 Flyback Diode
The Inductive Kickback Absorber
Exact Placement: Connect in reverse bias directly across the two terminals of your 12V Solenoid / Mag Lock (or into the Relay screw terminal block between NO and GND).
[SILVER BAND] → Connect to Lock (+) / Relay NO
[BLACK END] → Connect to Lock (-) / GND

⚠️ Never reverse this! The silver band MUST face positive (+12V). If wired backward, it will cause a short-circuit.

2
1000µF 16V Capacitor
The Anti-Brownout Energy Reservoir
Exact Placement: Connect directly across the 5V OUT (+) and GND (-) output pins of your LM2596 buck converter (or across the 5V power rails on your breadboard right next to ESP32 VIN).
[LONG LEAD (+)] → Connect to 5V Rail (ESP32 VIN)
[SHORT LEAD / STRIPE (-)] → Connect to Common GND

Provides instant stored energy when the relay coil pulls current, keeping the ESP32 power line above 4.8V.

3
Relay Module & JD-VCC
100% Optical Isolation from Lock Noise
Option A (Standard): Keep JD-VCC jumper on. Connect Relay VCC to 5V, GND to GND, and IN to GPIO 27.

Option B (Pro Isolation): Remove the black jumper cap from JD-VCC!
[JD-VCC Pin] → Connect to 5V Buck Converter
[VCC Pin] → Connect to ESP32 3V3 Pin
[IN Pin] → Connect to ESP32 GPIO 27
This completely isolates the ESP32 microchip electrically from the mechanical relay coil.
