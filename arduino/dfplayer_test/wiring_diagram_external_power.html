✅ Why Dual 1kΩ Resistors & External Power Protect Your ESP32

External Power: Prevents flash read err, 988 boot loops by isolating the MP3 module's heavy power draw from the ESP32 USB rail.
TX Resistor (GPIO 17 -> MP3 RX): Dampens switching noise and limits input current to the DFPlayer RX pin.
RX Resistor (MP3 TX -> GPIO 16): Limits current and acts as a protective buffer between the 5V logic output of the DFPlayer TX pin and the 3.3V sensitive ESP32 GPIO 16 pin, preventing over-voltage damage to your ESP32 board!
