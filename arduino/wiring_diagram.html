Crucial Beginner Setup Rules:

1. RC522 RFID Must Connect to 3.3V ONLY: Connecting RC522 to 5V will permanently fry its chip.
2. Calibrate Buck Converter First: Turn the brass screw on the LM2596 until your multimeter reads exactly 5.0V on Row 58 before connecting the ESP32.
3. Common Ground Is Mandatory: Breadboard Rows 30, 33, and 40 are bridged to form one shared Common Ground.

📋 Step-by-Step Breadboard Sequence
  • 1
    Setup 5V & GND Rails

    Buck VOUT+ to G1 Row 58, Buck VOUT- to G1 Row 30. Add jumpers: Row 58➔Row 50, and Row 30➔Row 33➔Row 40.

  • 2
    Install 1000µF Smoothing Capacitor

    Positive leg into G2 Row 5 (jumper to Row 58), Negative leg into G2 Row 10 (jumper to Row 30).

  • 3
    Connect ESP32 Power & Ground

    ESP32 5V (VIN) into G1 Row 50. ESP32 GND into G1 Row 40.

  • 4
    Wire DS3231 RTC Clock

    VCC to Row 50, GND to Row 40. SDA to G2 Row 30 (GPIO 21), SCL to G2 Row 28 (GPIO 22).

  • 5
    Wire RFID RC522 (3.3V Only!)

    RC522 3.3V to ESP32 3V3 pin. GND to Row 40. SS➔G5, SCK➔G18, MOSI➔G23, MISO➔G19, RST➔G4.

  • 6
    Wire DFPlayer Mini with 1kΩ Resistors

    VCC to Row 50, GND to Row 40. GPIO 17 ➔ 1kΩ ➔ RX (Pin 2). TX (Pin 3) ➔ 1kΩ ➔ GPIO 16. Speaker to Pins 6 & 8.
