Assembly Step-by-Step Instructions
1

Inline 1kΩ Resistor

Place a 1kΩ resistor between ESP32 GPIO 17 (TX2) and DFPlayer Pin 2 (RX) to buffer 3.3V to 5V logic and eliminate serial speaker noise.

2

Direct Speaker Connection

Plug the JST-PH 2-Pin connector from your 3W 8Ω speaker directly into DFPlayer Pins 6 (SPK1) and 8 (SPK2). No external audio amplifier needed!

3

5V Power & Common Ground

Power DFPlayer VCC (Pin 1) from the LM2596 5V output rail. Ensure ESP32, DFPlayer, and Relay share a common GND.
